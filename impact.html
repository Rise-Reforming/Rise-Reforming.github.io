Budget-positive tech that eliminates GHGs and plastic waste for a sustainable business future

We source plastic directly from retailers and manufacturers, repurposing plastic that would otherwise end up in a landfill or incinerated. The current process is costly for these businesses. In one case, a company reported paying $50/ton to landfill and $500/ton to incinerate their plastic waste streams. We would take this plastic for free, reducing the significant costs of plastic waste disposal.

Rise Reforming will harness the virtually limitless resource of plastic waste to significantly reduce its generation in the US while also lowering greenhouse gas emissions:

23 million metric tons of CO2 is the equivalent of the annual emissions from five million cars.
