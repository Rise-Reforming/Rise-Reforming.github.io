Budget-positive tech that eliminates GHGs and plastic waste for a sustainable business future

We source plastic directly from retailers and manufacturers, repurposing plastic that would otherwise end up in a landfill or incinerated. The current process is costly for these businesses. In one case, a company reported paying $50/ton to landfill and $500/ton to incinerate.
